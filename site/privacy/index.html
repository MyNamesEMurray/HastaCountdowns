Privacy Policy

Effective October 2, 2026

The short version: Hasta does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything you create stays on your iPhone.

Information we collect

None. Hasta has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code. We never see your countdowns, photos, or any information about you or your device.

Data stored on your device

The countdowns you create, along with their names, dates, colors, symbols, and settings, are saved only on your iPhone, inside storage shared between the Hasta app and its widgets. This data is included in your device backups according to your own iCloud or computer backup settings, which Apple manages. Deleting Hasta deletes this data.

Photos

If you choose a photo as a widget background, Hasta uses the system photo picker, which shares only the photo you select. Hasta saves a resized copy on your device for your widgets. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. Hasta does not use push notification servers.

Purchases

Hasta Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. The app checks your purchase status directly with Apple on your device.

Children

Hasta does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If Hasta ever begins collecting data, this page will say so before it happens.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@hasta.day.
