Privacy Policy

Updated October 2, 2026

The short version: Hasta does not collect or share your data. The only exception is the optional Live Activity notifications described below. Everything you create stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud Sync, in your own private iCloud account, which we can't access.

Information we collect

None, except for the optional Live Activity notifications described below. Hasta has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking code. We never see your countdowns, photos, or any information about you or your device.

Data stored on your device

The countdowns you create, along with their names, dates, colors, symbols, and settings, are saved only on your iPhone, inside storage shared between the Hasta app and its widgets. This data is included in your device backups according to your own iCloud or computer backup settings, which Apple manages. Deleting Hasta deletes this data.

iCloud Sync

iCloud Sync is on by default and can be turned off in Hasta's Settings. When it's on, your countdowns, reminders, and background photos are stored in the private database of your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit, so they appear on your other devices and return when you set up a new one. This data is protected by your Apple Account and Apple's iCloud security. We have no access to it, and it's never shared with us or anyone else.

Photos

If you choose a photo as a widget background, Hasta uses the system photo picker, which shares only the photo you select. Hasta saves a resized copy on your device for your widgets. Photos are never uploaded anywhere except your own private iCloud account when iCloud Sync is on.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally by iOS on your device.

Live Activity notifications

If you have Hasta Premium and leave "Start Live Activities" on in Settings, Hasta sends the event time and a random ID for each of your upcoming countdowns, plus Apple's push token for your device, to Hasta's notification server. It never sends countdown names, symbols, colors, photos or any account information; the name and look of the Live Activity are filled in on your iPhone. The server uses this data only to send a notification that starts the Live Activity before the event, and it deletes it when you turn the setting off or after 40 days without an update. It never links it to your identity and never shares it. Like any web server, our hosting provider may see your IP address in short-lived request logs. Turning the setting off sends a request that removes your data from the server.

Purchases

Hasta Premium is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment, and we do not receive your name, email address, or payment details. The app checks your purchase status directly with Apple on your device.

Children

Hasta does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If Hasta ever begins collecting data, this page will say so before it happens.

Contact
